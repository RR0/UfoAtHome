3D decor (rr0-scene)

A sky/horizon/starfield backdrop computed from each sighting's real reported time and place — four real, documented cases spanning night, dawn, midday and dusk. Astronomically accurate: Sun/Moon/planet positions, the Moon's phase, and a real star catalog (magnitude ≤ 7.5) for the given instant and location.

The last four entries are not cases and hold no recorded shape at all: they are skies set up with the conditions one sight needs, for looking at that sight. A rainbow wants rain, a Sun low enough for the bow to clear the horizon, a gap in the cloud for the light to come through, and a witness facing away from the Sun — all four, or there is nothing to see. The Milky Way and the zodiacal light are the same kind of conjunction and it is worth knowing which conditions they are: no Moon at all, a Sun far enough down, a clear sky, and the plane in question standing high rather than lying on the horizon.
